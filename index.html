Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · FinanceMe

Key Promise: FinanceMe respects your absolute financial privacy. We never sell your personal information, monetize your transactions, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the data you explicitly choose to enter into FinanceMe:

  • Account Profiles: Your email address and optional display name when creating an account or logging in via Google.
  • Financial Entries: Account balances, transaction titles, dates, amounts, categories, and personal notes.
  • Debt & Credit Facilities: Loan balances, original principal, APR rates, minimum payments, and credit card limits.
  • Goals & Subscriptions: Savings targets, subscription renewal dates, and recurring cadence.

2. How Your Data is Stored & Secured

When unauthenticated, your data is saved strictly to your local browser (LocalStorage). When signed in, records are synchronized in real-time to your private Google Cloud Firebase Firestore database under strict row-level security rules enforcing request.auth.uid == userId. No other user can read or modify your data.

3. Third-Party Integrations

Authentication is powered by Google Firebase Auth. We do not use third-party behavioral trackers, session recorders, or ad network cookies.

4. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You retain 100% ownership of your data at all times. You can:

  • Export: Download a full, unencrypted JSON backup of all your financial ledgers and settings at any time via the Backup & Restore menu.
  • Permanently Delete: Use the "Delete My Account & Data" button in your Profile Settings or Footer to instantly and irreversibly erase all your records from both Firestore and local storage.

5. Updates & Contact

Any future revisions to this policy will be clearly posted within the app. If you have questions regarding your privacy, contact support or review your project settings directly.
